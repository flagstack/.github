# Switch On Your Code Code of Conduct

Switch On Your Code is committed to providing a welcoming, professional, and harassment-free community for everyone.

This policy is based on the principles of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.

## Expected behaviour

Community members should:

- treat other people with respect and consideration;
- give and receive technical feedback constructively;
- respect differing viewpoints, backgrounds, and levels of experience;
- focus criticism on ideas and code rather than people;
- accept responsibility for mistakes and correct them where appropriate;
- help keep discussions productive and relevant to the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

The following are not acceptable in Switch On Your Code community spaces:

- harassment, intimidation, or discrimination;
- insulting, derogatory, or demeaning comments;
- sexualised language, imagery, or unwanted sexual attention;
- trolling or deliberate disruption of discussions;
- personal or political attacks directed at other participants;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- threats or encouragement of violence;
- conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to Switch On Your Code repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, community channels, events, and other spaces operated by the project. It also applies when someone is officially representing Switch On Your Code.

## Enforcement

Switch On Your Code maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate this policy. Depending on severity and history, enforcement may include a private warning, temporary restriction, or permanent removal from project community spaces.

Reports will be reviewed as confidentially and fairly as practical. Retaliation against someone making a good-faith report is not acceptable.

## Reporting conduct issues

If you need to report a Code of Conduct issue, contact a Switch On Your Code maintainer privately rather than opening a public issue. A dedicated community contact method will be published as the project grows.
